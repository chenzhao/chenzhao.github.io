Key concept / 核心概念

Entropy / 熵

Entropy is a careful way to count how many microscopic arrangements support the same visible description. 熵不是一句模糊的“混乱”，而是在计数同一宏观描述背后的微观排列数量。

Definition / 定义

Boltzmann's formula \(S=k\log W\) says entropy grows with \(W\), the number of microstates compatible with a macrostate.

玻尔兹曼公式 \(S=k\log W\) 表示熵随 \(W\) 增长；\(W\) 是与某个宏观状态相容的微观态数量。

\[ W=\binom{N}{K},\qquad S=k\log W \]

Example / 例子

If 10 particles can be on or off, the macrostate "5 are on" has many more realizations than "0 are on." That is why middle counts tend to be typical.

如果 10 个粒子各自可以开或关，那么“5 个打开”的宏观态比“0 个打开”有更多实现方式，所以中间值通常更典型。

Why it matters / 为什么重要

Entropy explains why probability is not only about a single state's energy. A macro-description can dominate because many microstates point to it.

熵说明概率不只看某一个状态的能量。一个宏观描述可能因为背后有大量微观态而自然占优势。
