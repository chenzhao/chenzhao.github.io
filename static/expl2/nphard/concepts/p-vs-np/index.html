P vs NP

P vs NP 询问所有能快速验证的问题，是否也都能快速求解。P vs NP asks whether every efficiently checkable problem is also efficiently solvable.

\[\mathrm{P}\subseteq\mathrm{NP},\qquad \mathrm{P}\stackrel{?}{=}\mathrm{NP}\]

例子 / Example

子集和的候选答案容易检查，但是否总能快速找到这样的候选答案，正是这类问题的直觉核心。

Subset-sum candidates are easy to check, but whether such candidates can always be found quickly is the intuition behind the question.

关系 / Relations

前置：P、NP、证据、NP-hard。后继：密码学、优化、自动证明等领域的深层影响。

Upstream: P, NP, certificate, NP-hard. Downstream: deep consequences for cryptography, optimization, automated proof, and more.
