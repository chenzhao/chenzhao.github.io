Concept / 概念

Rule-following / 遵守规则

Rules gain force in trained, correctable practice. 规则的力量体现在受训练、可纠正的实践中。

Definition / 定义

Following a rule is not merely consulting a private interpretation; it is being able to go on in a way the practice recognizes.

遵守规则不只是查阅私人解释，而是能够以实践承认的方式继续下去。

Example / 例子

After 2, 4, 6, 8, the normal continuation of "add 2" is 10 because training and correction have fixed how to go on.

在 2, 4, 6, 8 之后，“加 2”的正常延续是 10，因为训练和纠正固定了怎样继续。

Dependencies / 依赖

Upstream: private language. Downstream: form of life.

上游：私人语言。下游：生活形式。
