归约 / Reduction

归约把一个问题高效翻译成另一个问题，用来比较问题难度。A reduction efficiently translates one problem into another to compare difficulty.

\[A\le_p B\iff \exists f\in\mathrm{polytime},\ x\in A\iff f(x)\in B\]

例子 / Example

如果 SAT 可以多项式时间翻译成子集和，那么一个通用快速子集和算法也会给 SAT 带来快速算法。

If SAT reduces to subset sum in polynomial time, a general fast subset-sum solver would also give SAT a fast solver.

关系 / Relations

前置：P、NP。后继：NP-hard。Upstream: P and NP. Downstream: NP-hard.
