Boltzmannia

A continuous reading version from thermodynamics to Boltzmann machines. 这是一份从热力学到玻尔兹曼机的连续阅读版。

Chapter 0 / 第 0 章

Start with a landscape / 先从地形开始

Imagine a ball on uneven terrain. It tends to stay in valleys, but heat can still jostle it up a slope. This is the intuitive picture behind Boltzmannia: probability is not arbitrary; it is shaped by energy, temperature, and the number of microscopic ways a visible situation can happen.

想象一个小球落在起伏地形上。它倾向于停在低谷，但热扰动仍可能把它推上斜坡。Boltzmannia 的直觉就是这样： 概率不是随意分配的，而是由能量、温度，以及同一个可见情形背后的微观实现方式共同塑造。

Chapter 1 / 第一章

Thermal equilibrium / 热平衡

The Boltzmann distribution says a state with energy \(E\) receives weight \(e^{-E/(kT)}\). Low-energy states are favored, high-energy states remain possible, and temperature controls how sharp the preference is.

玻尔兹曼分布说，能量为 \(E\) 的状态得到权重 \(e^{-E/(kT)}\)。 低能状态更受偏爱，高能状态仍有机会出现，而温度控制这种偏爱的强弱。

\[ P(E)=Z^{-1}e^{-E/(kT)} \]
Temperature / 温度

Definition: temperature sets the tolerance for high-energy states. Example: when the slider is high, high-energy levels remain visible. Why it matters: the same idea becomes sampling temperature in the machine.

定义：温度设置系统对高能状态的容忍度。例子：滑块越高，高能级越可见。重要性：同一思想会变成机器中的抽样温度。

Lower setting: sharper low-energy preference. Higher setting: flatter exploration. 低值代表更强低能偏好，高值代表更平坦探索。

Chapter 2 / 第二章

Entropy and microstates / 熵与微观态

Entropy, written as \(S=k\log W\), counts microscopic realizations. If many detailed arrangements all look like the same macrostate, that macrostate becomes typical even before we mention learning.

熵可写成 \(S=k\log W\)，它计数微观实现方式。如果很多具体排列都看起来像同一个宏观状态， 这个宏观状态就会自然变得典型。

\[ W=\binom{N}{K},\qquad S=k\log W \]
Entropy / 熵

Definition: entropy is the logarithmic count of microstates behind a macrostate. Example: five active particles among ten has more arrangements than zero active particles.

定义：熵是某个宏观状态背后微观态数量的对数计数。例子：十个粒子中五个打开，比零个打开有更多排列方式。

Middle counts usually have more combinations. 中间值通常有更多组合方式。

Chapter 3 / 第三章

Boltzmann machines / 玻尔兹曼机

A Boltzmann machine transfers this language to neural networks. Visible units represent data, hidden units represent latent explanations, and weights define an energy for every configuration. The model samples states according to the probabilities implied by those energies.

玻尔兹曼机把这套语言迁移到神经网络。可见单元表示数据，隐藏单元表示潜在解释，权重为每个组合状态定义能量； 模型再按这些能量隐含的概率进行抽样。

\[ E(v,h)=-b^\top v-c^\top h-v^\top Wh,\qquad p(v,h)\propto e^{-E(v,h)/T} \]
Boltzmann machine / 玻尔兹曼机

Definition: a probabilistic neural model that gives configurations an energy score. Example: two visible units can learn that 00 and 11 are more data-like than mismatched pairs.

定义：给神经元组合状态分配能量分数的概率模型。例子：两个可见单元可以学到 00 和 11 比不匹配组合更像数据。

Chapter 4 / 第四章

Learning the landscape / 学习能量景观

Training edits the terrain. The positive phase lowers states seen in real data; the negative phase looks at the model's own samples and pushes back against unsupported states that became too probable.

训练是在编辑地形。正相压低真实数据中的状态，负相查看模型自己的抽样，并纠正那些缺乏数据支持却概率过高的状态。

Energy landscape / 能量景观

Definition: an assignment of energy height to every state. Example: learning lowers data states into valleys and raises unsupported states relative to them.

定义：给每个状态分配能量高度。例子：学习会把数据状态压成低谷，并相对抬高缺乏支持的状态。

As learning progresses, data-like states become deeper valleys. 随着学习推进，数据式状态会变成更深的低谷。

Concept index / 概念索引

Four anchors / 四个锚点

  • Temperature / 温度: exploration tolerance for high-energy states.
  • Entropy / 熵: count of microscopic realizations.
  • Energy landscape / 能量景观: terrain of state energies.
  • Boltzmann machine / 玻尔兹曼机: learned probabilistic energy model.

Takeaway / 收束

One line of thought / 一条思想线

Boltzmannia is about a reusable pattern: many possible states exist, energy makes some states easier to occupy, entropy explains why some descriptions are typical, and learning can reshape the energy map so useful patterns become easier to sample.

Boltzmannia 关注的是一个可复用模式：许多状态同时存在，能量让某些状态更容易出现，熵解释为什么某些描述更典型， 学习则能改造能量地图，让有用模式更容易被抽样。
