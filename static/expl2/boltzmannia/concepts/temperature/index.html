Key concept / 核心概念

Temperature / 温度

Temperature is the knob that decides how much high-energy states are still allowed to appear. 温度是一个旋钮：它决定高能状态还剩多少出现机会。

Definition / 定义

In the Boltzmann distribution, probability is weighted by \(e^{-E/(kT)}\). If \(T\) is small, a modest energy increase is punished strongly. If \(T\) is large, the same energy increase matters less.

在玻尔兹曼分布中，概率权重是 \(e^{-E/(kT)}\)。温度低时，小小的能量升高也会被强烈压低；温度高时，同样的能量差影响变弱。

\[ p_i=\frac{e^{-E_i/(kT)}}{\sum_j e^{-E_j/(kT)}} \]

Example / 例子

On the main page, move the temperature slider. Low temperature concentrates probability in the first energy level; high temperature spreads probability across several levels.

在主页面移动温度滑块：低温时概率集中在最低能级，高温时概率会铺到多个能级。

Why it matters / 为什么重要

The same concept reappears in Boltzmann machines as sampling temperature. It controls exploration: too cold and the model gets stuck, too hot and it ignores the landscape.

这个概念会在玻尔兹曼机里变成抽样温度。它控制探索：太冷容易卡在低谷，太热又会忽略地形。
