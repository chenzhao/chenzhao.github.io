5. 自由能 / Free energy

把能量和熵放进同一个账本 / Put energy and entropy on one ledger

自由能提醒读者：系统不只偏爱低能量，也偏爱拥有许多微观实现的状态。Free energy ties together the pull of low energy and the pull of many realizations.

展开原理 / Expand

低能量像低谷，熵像通往某个区域的道路数量。一个宏观状态即使能量略高，只要背后的微观道路很多，也可能在热平衡中占据显著概率。机器学习里的能量模型也有类似权衡：模型要压低数据附近的能量，同时避免把概率全部挤进少数脆弱状态。

Low energy is a valley, while entropy counts how many roads lead into a region. A slightly higher-energy macrostate can still matter if it has many realizations. Energy-based learning faces a related balance: lower the data region, but avoid a brittle probability map.

\[ F=E-TS,\qquad P(\text{macro})\propto e^{-F/(kT)} \]
视觉账本 / Visual ledger

\(E\downarrow\) and \(S\uparrow\) both push probability weight upward.

6. 抽样 / Sampling

知道概率还不等于容易抽到 / Knowing probabilities is not easy sampling

当状态空间很大时，模型往往不能枚举全部状态，只能用随机游走近似探索。Large spaces require approximate wandering instead of complete enumeration.

展开原理 / Expand

玻尔兹曼分布给每个状态一个权重，但真实模型可能有天文数量的状态。抽样算法像在能量地形上行走：低温时容易困在局部低谷，高温时能探索更远但也更嘈杂。这就是为什么温度、马尔可夫链和近似训练总是一起出现。

The Boltzmann distribution assigns weights, but realistic models have enormous state spaces. Sampling behaves like walking over the terrain: low temperature can trap the walk in a local valley, while high temperature explores farther with more noise.

\[ \alpha(x\to y)=\min\left(1,e^{-(E(y)-E(x))/(kT)}\right) \]
抽样路径 / Sampling path

\(\text{start}\to y\to \alpha(x\to y)\to \text{next}\)

7. 受限模型 / Restricted models

限制连接是为了让学习可计算 / Restrictions make learning computable

受限玻尔兹曼机会删掉同层连接，让可见层和隐藏层之间的条件计算变简单。RBMs remove within-layer links so conditional updates become tractable.

展开原理 / Expand

完全连接的玻尔兹曼机表达能力强，但抽样和训练压力很大。受限玻尔兹曼机牺牲一部分连接结构，换来更容易交替更新可见层和隐藏层的算法。这是建模中的常见交易：结构越自由，计算越困难；结构越受限，算法越清楚。

A fully connected Boltzmann machine is expressive but hard to train. A restricted Boltzmann machine gives up some connections so visible and hidden layers can be updated more cleanly. Model design often trades freedom for computation.

\[ E(v,h)=-a^\top v-b^\top h-v^\top W h \]
二分结构 / Bipartite structure

\(v\leftrightarrow h\), with no \(v\text{-}v\) or \(h\text{-}h\) links.

8. 现代连接 / Modern connection

能量语言仍然帮助理解生成模型 / Energy language still helps generative models

即使今天常见的是扩散模型和 Transformer，能量景观仍能解释“哪些状态合理、哪些状态应被压低”。Even newer models can be read as shaping what configurations are plausible.

展开原理 / Expand

现代生成模型不一定显式写出玻尔兹曼机的能量函数，但它们仍在学习数据附近的高概率区域。能量语言提供了一张共同地图：训练塑造概率地形，采样沿着地形寻找合理状态，验证则检查生成结果是否落在数据支持的区域。

Modern generators may not expose a Boltzmann-machine energy function, yet they still learn high-probability regions near data. The energy metaphor keeps one map: training shapes terrain, sampling searches it, and evaluation checks whether generated states are supported.

\[ p_\theta(x)\propto e^{-E_\theta(x)} \]
共同地图 / Shared map

\(\text{data}\to E_\theta(x)\to p_\theta(x)\to \text{sample}\)
