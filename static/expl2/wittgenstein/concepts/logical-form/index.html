Concept / 概念

Logical Form / 逻辑形式

Logical form is the structural condition that lets proposition and fact be compared. 逻辑形式是使命题与事实能够比较的结构条件。

Definition / 定义

In the early view, logical form is shown in the proposition's capacity to picture a fact; it is not one more fact inside the world.

在早期观点中，逻辑形式显示在命题描画事实的能力中；它不是世界中的另一个事实。

Example / 例子

A scale model and a real room can be compared because their parts can be projected onto each other by a shared arrangement.

比例模型和真实房间能够比较，是因为它们的部分可以通过共享排列投射到彼此。

Dependencies / 依赖

Upstream: picture theory. Downstream: early silence about what can only be shown.

上游：图像论。下游：早期关于“只能显示者”的沉默。
