Concept dependency tree / 概念依赖图

Wittgenstein's Philosophy / 维特根斯坦哲学

Concepts move from early logical depiction to later social practice. 概念路线从早期逻辑描画走向后期社会实践。

1 / early

Picture theory / 图像论

Definition: A proposition pictures a possible fact by sharing structure with it.

定义：命题通过共享结构来描画可能事实。

Upstream: Chapter 0 route. Downstream: logical form, limits of saying.

2 / condition

Logical form / 逻辑形式

Definition: The structural condition that lets proposition and fact be compared.

定义：使命题与事实可比较的结构条件。

Upstream: picture theory. Downstream: the early boundary between saying and showing.

3 / turn

Language-game / 语言游戏

Definition: A situated, rule-governed linguistic activity.

定义：处在具体情境中的规则化语言活动。

Upstream: limits of picture theory. Downstream: meaning as use, form of life.

4 / use

Meaning as use / 意义即使用

Definition: A word's meaning is often understood through its role in practice.

定义：许多词的意义要从它在实践中的角色理解。

Upstream: language-game. Downstream: family resemblance, private language, therapy.

5 / criteria

Private language / 私人语言

Definition: A hypothetical language fixed only by private inner experience.

定义：一种只靠私人内在经验固定意义的假想语言。

Upstream: meaning as use. Downstream: rule-following and public criteria.

6 / norm

Rule-following / 遵守规则

Definition: Continuing according to a rule through trained, correctable practice.

定义：在受训练、可纠正的实践中按规则继续。

Upstream: private language problem. Downstream: form of life.

7 / ground

Form of life / 生活形式

Definition: The shared background in which language-games can be learned and continued.

定义：语言游戏可以被学习和延续的共享生活背景。

Upstream: language-game, rule-following. Downstream: philosophical therapy.
