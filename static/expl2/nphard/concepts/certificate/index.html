证据 / Certificate

证据是一份候选答案的短说明，让验证器能快速检查答案是否成立。A certificate is a short witness that lets a verifier check a candidate answer quickly.

例子 / Example

目标 22 的子集和里，10 与 12 就是一份证据，因为 \(10+12=22\)。

For target 22 in subset sum, 10 and 12 form a certificate because \(10+12=22\).

\[\sum_{i\in S}a_i=10+12=22\]
