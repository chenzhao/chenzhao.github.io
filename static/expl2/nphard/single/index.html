P、NP 与 P vs NP

这是一份线性阅读版本，把核心章节和概念定义放在同一页。 This linear version keeps the core chapters and concept definitions on one page.

0. 先看直觉 / Intuition first

朋友说可以用几张优惠券正好凑出 22 元。给出优惠券后，检查很快；什么都不给时，寻找组合可能很慢。

A friend says some coupons add exactly to 22. Checking the handed-over coupons is quick; finding them from scratch may be slow.

新版主线：先把任务建模成判定问题，再分清可计算、可高效求解、可高效验证，最后用归约比较难度。

The revised path is: model the task as a decision problem, separate computable from efficiently solvable and efficiently checkable, then compare difficulty with reductions.

概念定义 / Concept definitions

  • 可判定 / Decidable: 有算法总会停下来并回答。An algorithm always halts and answers yes/no.
  • P: 已知多项式时间可解。Known solvable in polynomial time.
  • NP: “是”的答案有短证据可快速验证。Yes answers have short certificates verifiable quickly.
  • 证据 / Certificate: 让验证器快速检查的候选答案说明。A short witness checked by a verifier.
  • 归约 / Reduction: 高效地把一个问题翻译成另一个问题。An efficient translation between problems.
  • NP-hard: 至少和 NP 中所有问题一样难。At least as hard as every problem in NP.
  • P vs NP: 快速验证是否意味着快速求解？Does efficiently checkable imply efficiently solvable?
\[\mathrm{P}=\bigcup_{k\ge1}\mathrm{TIME}(n^k),\qquad \mathrm{P}\subseteq\mathrm{NP}\]

1. 可计算理论 / Computability

可计算理论先问是否存在通用算法。停机问题说明有些问题不是慢，而是没有覆盖所有输入的判定算法。

Computability first asks whether a general algorithm exists. The halting problem shows that some problems are not merely slow; no universal decision algorithm exists.

可判定语言满足：对每个输入 \(x\)，算法 \(A(x)\) 停机并正确判断 \(x\in L\)。

停机问题的反证骨架：若有 \(H(P,x)\)，就构造一个程序 \(D(P)\) 专门反着做；再问 \(D(D)\) 会导致矛盾。

Halting proof sketch: if \(H(P,x)\) existed, build \(D(P)\) to do the opposite; asking about \(D(D)\) creates a contradiction.

2. P 与增长率 / P and growth

P 用多项式时间刻画高效。\(n^2\)、\(n^3\) 会增长，但比 \(2^n\) 和 \(n!\) 温和得多。

P formalizes efficiency as polynomial time. \(n^2\) and \(n^3\) grow, but far more gently than \(2^n\) and \(n!\).

增长比较写作 \(n^2,n^3,2^n,n!\)，核心是 \(n\to\infty\) 时谁更快变大。

注意输入规模：子集和的 \(O(nT)\) 算法对数值 \(T\) 是多项式，但对 \(T\) 的二进制长度可能是指数级。

Mind input size: subset-sum \(O(nT)\) is polynomial in numeric \(T\), but can be exponential in the bit-length of \(T\).

3. NP 与证据 / NP and certificates

NP 的入门直觉是：答案若为“是”，就有短证据可快速检查。子集和中，被选数字就是证据。

The first intuition for NP is that yes answers have short certificates. In subset sum, the selected numbers are the certificate.

\[x\in L\iff \exists y,\ |y|\le |x|^c\land V(x,y)=1\]

SAT 的证据是一组变量赋值；哈密顿路径的证据是一串顶点顺序。二者都说明“短证据”和“验证器”是 NP 的核心。

For SAT the certificate is an assignment; for Hamiltonian path it is an ordered vertex list. Both show why certificates and verifiers define NP.

4. NP-hard 与归约 / NP-hardness and reductions

归约把一个问题翻译成另一个问题。若所有 NP 问题都能翻译到 X，X 就是 NP-hard。

A reduction translates one problem into another. If every NP problem reduces to X, then X is NP-hard.

\[A\le_p B\iff \exists f\in\mathrm{polytime},\ x\in A\iff f(x)\in B\]

方向很重要：要证明 B 难，必须从已知困难的 A 出发证明 \(A\le_p B\)。

Direction matters: to prove B hard, start from a known-hard A and show \(A\le_p B\).

5. P vs NP

P 包含在 NP 里；未知的是 NP 是否也包含在 P 里。大多数研究者猜想 \(\mathrm{P}\ne\mathrm{NP}\)。

P is contained in NP; the open question is whether NP is contained in P. Most researchers conjecture \(\mathrm{P}\ne\mathrm{NP}\).

对子集和，如果有快速判定器，可以反复询问保留/删去某个数后是否仍有解，从而构造一个解。

For subset sum, a fast decider can be queried repeatedly while keeping or dropping numbers to construct an actual solution.

6. NP-complete

NP-complete 问题既属于 NP，又是 NP-hard。它们的“是”答案可快速验证，同时足够代表整个 NP 家族的困难。

NP-complete problems are both in NP and NP-hard. Their yes answers are quickly checkable, and their difficulty represents the whole NP family.

\[X\in\mathrm{NP}\text{-}\mathrm{complete}\iff X\in\mathrm{NP}\land \forall L\in\mathrm{NP},L\le_p X\]

SAT 是第一个 NP-complete 问题；常见归约链条包括 SAT → 3-SAT → Clique → Vertex Cover → Hamiltonian Cycle。

SAT was the first NP-complete problem; a common reduction chain is SAT → 3-SAT → Clique → Vertex Cover → Hamiltonian Cycle.

7. 为什么重要 / Why it matters

P vs NP 影响密码、优化、排程、程序验证和自动证明，因为这些领域常依赖“验证容易、寻找困难”的差别。

P vs NP touches cryptography, optimization, scheduling, program verification, and automated proof because these areas often rely on the gap between easy checking and hard finding.

排考场、芯片验证、自动找证明都常见这种模式：给出方案后检查很快，但从零搜索方案可能巨大。

Exam scheduling, chip verification, and automated proof all share the pattern: checking a proposal is fast, searching from scratch may be huge.

8. 证明要什么 / What proof needs

一个算法在若干例子上跑得快，不足以证明 \(\mathrm{P}=\mathrm{NP}\)；长期没有找到快速算法，也不足以证明 \(\mathrm{P}\ne\mathrm{NP}\)。

An algorithm running quickly on some examples does not prove \(\mathrm{P}=\mathrm{NP}\); not finding a fast algorithm for a long time does not prove \(\mathrm{P}\ne\mathrm{NP}\).

SAT solver 在工业例子上强，不等于所有 SAT 都有多项式算法；相对化、自然证明、代数化等障碍提示常规证明路线不够。

SAT solvers being strong on industrial instances does not imply polynomial time for all SAT; barriers such as relativization, natural proofs, and algebrization show why standard proof routes are insufficient.

9. 结论 / Takeaway

能算、好算、好检查不是同一件事。P vs NP 正是把“我能确认答案”和“我能快速找到答案”分开。

Computable, efficiently solvable, and efficiently checkable are different. P vs NP separates confirming an answer from finding one quickly.

\[\text{decidable}\supseteq\mathrm{NP}\supseteq\mathrm{P},\qquad \mathrm{P}\stackrel{?}{=}\mathrm{NP}\]

完整例子：旅行商优化版问最短巡回路线多长，是 NP-hard；判定版问是否存在长度不超过 K 的路线，是 NP-complete。

Full example: TSP optimization asks for the shortest tour and is NP-hard; the decision version asks whether a tour of length at most K exists and is NP-complete.
