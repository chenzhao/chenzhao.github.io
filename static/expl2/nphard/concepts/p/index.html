P

P 是已知可以在多项式时间内求解的一类判定问题。P is the class of decision problems known to be solvable in polynomial time.

\[\mathrm{P}=\bigcup_{k\ge1}\mathrm{TIME}(n^k)\]

例子 / Example

排序、最短路径、最大流都有多项式时间算法。它们未必在所有输入上瞬间完成，但增长方式可控。

Sorting, shortest path, and maximum flow have polynomial-time algorithms. They are not always instant, but their growth is controlled.

关系 / Relations
