Concept / 概念

Picture Theory / 图像论

A proposition has sense by picturing a possible fact through shared structure. 命题通过共享结构描画可能事实，从而具有意义。

Definition / 定义

Early Wittgenstein's picture theory says that a meaningful proposition is a model of a possible state of affairs.

早期维特根斯坦的图像论认为，有意义的命题是某种可能事态的模型。

Example / 例子

A map pictures a city not by looking like buildings, but by preserving relations such as left, right, and connected roads.

地图描画城市不是因为它像建筑，而是因为它保存左右、连接道路等关系。
