Wittgenstein's Philosophy / 维特根斯坦哲学

Read from top to bottom: early picture theory, later language-games, and the therapeutic turn. 从头读到尾：早期图像论、后期语言游戏、 以及治疗式转向。

Chapter 0 / 第 0 章

One philosopher, two starting points / 同一个哲学家，两个出发点

Wittgenstein is easiest to understand as a route. Early Wittgenstein asks how language pictures the world; later Wittgenstein asks how language works inside life.

把维特根斯坦理解成一条路线最清楚。早期维特根斯坦问语言怎样描画世界；后期维特根斯坦问语言怎样在生活中运作。

The sentence "There is a brick here" can report a fact, while "Brick!" on a building site can function as an order. When the setting changes, the philosophical problem changes too.

“这里有一块砖”可以报告事实，而工地上的“砖！”可以作为命令发挥作用。场景一变，哲学问题也跟着变。

The Tractatus tries to draw the logical boundary between language, thought, and world. The Philosophical Investigations no longer looks for one hidden logical skeleton behind all meaning; it looks at language in action.

《逻辑哲学论》试图划出语言、思想和世界之间的逻辑边界。《哲学研究》则不再寻找所有意义背后的单一骨架，而是观察语言在活动中怎样运作。

Concept / 概念

Picture theory / 图像论

A proposition has sense by picturing a possible fact through shared structure. It need not resemble a photograph; a map or a scale model can picture because relations among parts can be projected.

命题通过共享结构来描画可能事实，因此具有意义。它不必像照片；地图和比例模型也能描画，因为部分之间的关系可以被投射。

Example / 例子

A map pictures a city not by looking like buildings, but by preserving left-right, distance, and connection relations. 地图描画城市不是因为它像建筑，而是因为它保存左右、距离和连接关系。

Chapter 1 / 第 1 章

Early propositions model possible facts / 早期命题像可能事实的模型

Early Wittgenstein thinks a meaningful proposition is a picture of a possible fact. The point is structural correspondence, not visual similarity.

早期维特根斯坦认为，有意义的命题是一幅可能事实的图。关键在于结构对应，而不是视觉相似。

When a proposition says "a is left of b," names and arrangement present one possible state. If the world is arranged that way, the proposition is true; if not, it is false.

当命题说“a 在 b 左边”时，名字和排列给出一种可能状态。世界若如此排列，命题为真；否则为假。

Meaning here comes from a possible comparison between proposition and world. The proposition is not merely a sound or sign; it is a model that can succeed or fail.

这里的意义来自命题与世界之间可能进行的比较。命题不只是声音或记号，它是一个可能成功也可能失败的模型。

Concept / 概念

Logical form / 逻辑形式

Logical form is the structural condition that lets proposition and fact be compared. It is not one more fact among facts, but the shared pattern that makes depiction possible.

逻辑形式是使命题与事实能够比较的结构条件。它不是事实中的另一个事实，而是让描画成为可能的共享模式。

Example / 例子

A room and its scale model can be compared because positions and relations in one can be projected onto the other. 房间和它的比例模型能比较，是因为位置和关系可以相互投射。

Chapter 2 / 第 2 章

What can be said, shown, and passed over / 能说、能显示、必须沉默

The Tractatus draws a boundary. Factual propositions can be said, while logical form is shown in meaningful propositions instead of being stated as another ordinary fact.

《逻辑哲学论》划出一条界限。事实命题可以说，而逻辑形式是在有意义命题中显示出来的，不是作为另一个普通事实被陈述。

That is why early Wittgenstein separates factual language from ethics, value, and the meaning of the world as a whole. These are not handled like simple reports.

这也是为什么早期维特根斯坦把事实语言与伦理、价值、世界整体意义分开。它们不是简单报告那样的东西。

The famous call for silence is not a claim that these matters are trivial. It is a claim that language has limits, and philosophy must notice where ordinary factual saying stops working.

著名的“保持沉默”并不是说这些事情不重要，而是说语言有界限，哲学必须注意普通事实语言在何处失效。

Concept / 概念

Language-game / 语言游戏

A language-game is a situated, rule-governed linguistic activity. Reporting, ordering, joking, calculating, praying, and proving are not one single operation wearing different costumes.

语言游戏是处在具体情境中的规则化语言活动。报告、命令、玩笑、计算、祈祷、证明并不是同一个操作穿着不同衣服。

Example / 例子

"Brick!" can be an order at a building site, a sample word in a classroom, and a label in a museum. “砖！”在工地上可以是命令，在课堂上可以是例词，在博物馆里可以是标签。

Chapter 3 / 第 3 章

Language does many kinds of work / 语言不只有一种工作

Later Wittgenstein turns away from one grand picture of all language. He looks instead at many language-games, each with its own rules, aims, and standards.

后期维特根斯坦离开了关于语言的单一大图景，转而看许多语言游戏，每一种都有自己的规则、目的和标准。

The same sentence-form can do different work in different settings. Philosophical confusion often starts when we ignore those differences and search for one hidden mechanism underneath them all.

同样的句子形式在不同场景里可以做不同工作。哲学混乱常从这里开始：我们忽略这些差异，想在它们下面寻找一个隐藏机制。

Language-game is therefore both descriptive and corrective. It describes how language actually functions, and it corrects the philosopher's temptation to overgeneralize.

因此，“语言游戏”既是描述性的，也是纠正性的。它描述语言怎样实际运作，也纠正哲学家过度概括的冲动。

Concept / 概念

Meaning as use / 意义即使用

In many cases, the meaning of a word is its use in the language. To understand a word is to know how it enters practice, how it is taught, how it is corrected, and what follows from saying it.

许多情况下，一个词的意义就在它在语言中的使用里。理解一个词，就是知道它怎样进入实践、怎样被教会、怎样被纠正，以及说出它之后会发生什么。

Example / 例子

"I know" works differently in an exam, in route-giving, and in first-person pain expression. “我知道”在考试、指路和第一人称疼痛表达中以不同方式工作。

Chapter 4 / 第 4 章

Do not first seek an inner object / 别先找内在对象

Meaning as use redirects a philosophical habit. Instead of first asking what object a word names, Wittgenstein asks how the word is actually used.

“意义即使用”纠正了一种哲学习惯。维特根斯坦不先问一个词指向什么对象，而先问它实际上怎样被使用。

This does not reduce meaning to shallow habit. Use includes authority, correction, consequence, training, and the place of a word inside a practice.

这并没有把意义降格成浅薄习惯。用法包含权威、纠正、后果、训练，以及一个词在实践中的位置。

That is why philosophical analysis often improves when it becomes patient description: who says this, when, under what conditions, and what counts as getting it right?

所以哲学分析常在变成耐心描述时更好：谁在说，何时说，在什么条件下说，什么又算说对了？

Chapter 5 / 第 5 章

Family resemblance / 家族相似

Some concepts do not share one strict essence. Wittgenstein uses "game" to show that members can be linked by overlapping similarities rather than one feature possessed by all.

有些概念并不共享一个严格本质。维特根斯坦用“游戏”说明，成员可以靠重叠相似联结，而不必都拥有同一个特征。

Board games, ball games, children's games, and card games overlap in shifting ways. One has competition and rules, another movement and scoring, another play and imitation.

棋类、球类、儿童游戏和纸牌游戏以变化的方式彼此重叠。一个强调竞争和规则，另一个强调动作和得分，再一个强调玩耍和模仿。

This matters because philosophy often expects definitions to uncover a hidden essence. Family resemblance reminds us that some concepts work through a network rather than a core.

这很重要，因为哲学常期待定义揭示隐藏本质。家族相似提醒我们，有些概念靠网络而不是核心来运作。

Concept / 概念

Private language / 私人语言

A fully private language would fix meanings only by private inner experience. Wittgenstein argues that this idea collapses because there is no stable criterion for correct use.

完全私人语言会只靠私人内在经验固定意义。维特根斯坦认为，这个想法会坍塌，因为它没有稳定的正确使用标准。

Example / 例子

If I mark a sensation "S" only by inwardly attending to it, later I cannot tell whether I am using it correctly or merely think I am. 如果我只靠内在注意把某种感觉标成“S”，以后就无法区分我是真的用对了，还是只是觉得自己用对了。

Chapter 6 / 第 6 章

Why fully private meaning cannot stand / 完全私有的意义为什么站不住

The private language argument does not deny pain or inner life. It questions whether meaning can be fixed only by a purely private act of inward pointing.

私人语言论证并不否认疼痛或内在生活。它质疑的是，意义是否能只靠一种纯粹私人的内在指认被固定。

If there is no public possibility of correction, then "correct use" threatens to collapse into "whatever seems right to me now." That is not enough to sustain a language.

如果没有公共纠正的可能，那么“正确使用”就会滑向“此刻对我来说好像是对的”。这不足以支撑一种语言。

Public criteria do not mean everyone has direct access to my feeling. They mean that language for feeling connects with expression, behavior, treatment, sympathy, and report.

公共标准并不意味着每个人都直接接触到我的感觉，而是说关于感觉的语言连接着表情、行为、治疗、同情和报告。

Concept / 概念

Rule-following / 遵守规则

Rules do not interpret themselves. To follow a rule is to continue in ways that training, correction, and shared practice sustain.

规则不会自己解释自己。遵守规则，就是以训练、纠正和共同实践能够维持的方式继续下去。

Example / 例子

After 2, 4, 6, 8 we write 10 because that is how the practice of "add 2" has taught us to continue. 在 2, 4, 6, 8 之后写 10，是因为“加 2”这项实践就是这样把我们训练成继续的。

Chapter 7 / 第 7 章

Rules do not carry their own rails / 规则不会自带轨道

A skeptic can always invent alternative rules that fit past examples. So the normativity of rules cannot come merely from a hidden private interpretation.

怀疑者总能发明符合过去样本的其他规则。因此，规则的规范性不能只来自隐藏的私人解释。

Wittgenstein's answer is practical. We learn to continue, correct, and agree inside a form of training. That background gives substance to the idea of going on correctly.

维特根斯坦的回答是实践性的。我们在一种训练形式里学会继续、纠正和一致，这个背景赋予“正确继续”以内容。

Rule-following therefore belongs with education, examples, drills, correction, and mutual recognition, not with a ghostly rail laid in the mind.

因此，遵守规则属于教育、例子、操练、纠正和相互承认，而不属于心中某条幽灵般铺好的轨道。

Concept / 概念

Form of life / 生活形式

A form of life is the shared background that lets language-games make sense: bodily reactions, habits, institutions, education, and worldly setting.

生活形式是让语言游戏能够成立的共享背景：身体反应、习惯、制度、教育以及所处世界。

Example / 例子

Counting works because people can point, repeat, correct, trade, teach, and agree on what counts as continuing correctly. 计数能够运作，是因为人们会指物、重复、纠正、交易、教学，并同意怎样才算正确继续。

Chapter 8 / 第 8 章

Language is rooted in shared life / 语言扎根在共享生活里

Forms of life are not an extra hidden metaphysics. They are the ordinary human background that makes words teachable, actions intelligible, and corrections possible.

生活形式不是额外的隐藏形而上学，它是让词能被教会、行动能被理解、纠正能发生的普通人类背景。

Children do not first master a private semantic theory and then enter social life. They learn words in activities, through training and response.

孩子不是先掌握一套私人语义理论再进入社会生活，而是在活动中、通过训练和回应学会词的。

That is why later Wittgenstein repeatedly sends us back to ordinary use. The ordinary is where language already has a home.

所以后期维特根斯坦反复把我们带回日常用法。日常正是语言已经拥有家园的地方。

Chapter 9 / 第 9 章

Philosophy unties knots / 哲学是在解结

Later Wittgenstein often treats philosophy as therapy. The aim is not always to build a theory, but to dissolve confusion by comparing uses and restoring context.

后期维特根斯坦常把哲学看成治疗。目标不总是建立理论，而是通过比较用法、恢复语境来解除混淆。

Questions such as "What is meaning really?" or "How does a rule force the future?" may already contain a misleading picture. Therapy asks where that picture came from.

像“意义究竟是什么？”“规则怎样强迫未来？”这样的问题，本身就可能包含误导性的图景。治疗会追问这个图景是怎样产生的。

This does not make philosophy trivial. It changes its ambition: philosophy can sort, separate, and clarify rather than always discover hidden entities.

这并没有让哲学变得琐碎，而是改变了它的雄心：哲学可以整理、区分和澄清，而不总是去发现隐藏实体。
