Concept / 概念

Form of Life / 生活形式

A form of life is the shared human background that lets language-games make sense. 生活形式是让语言游戏能够成立的共享生活背景。

Definition / 定义

Forms of life include bodily reactions, habits, education, institutions, and the world in which speech acts matter.

生活形式包括身体反应、习惯、教育、制度，以及言语行动能产生意义的世界。

Example / 例子

Counting works because people can point, repeat, correct, trade, teach, and agree on what counts as continuing correctly.

计数之所以可行，是因为人们会指物、重复、纠正、交易、教学，并同意怎样算正确继续。

Dependencies / 依赖

Upstream: language-games and rule-following. Downstream: philosophical therapy.

上游：语言游戏和遵守规则。下游：哲学治疗。
