NP-hard

NP-hard 表示至少和 NP 中所有问题一样难。NP-hard means at least as hard as every problem in NP.

\[X\ \text{is NP-hard}\iff \forall L\in\mathrm{NP},\ L\le_p X\]

例子 / Example

很多优化版问题是 NP-hard，但不一定属于 NP，因为 NP 主要讨论是/否判定问题。

Many optimization versions are NP-hard without necessarily being in NP, because NP mainly concerns yes/no decision problems.
