Key concept / 核心概念

Energy landscape / 能量景观

An energy landscape turns possible states into terrain: lower places are easier to occupy. 能量景观把可能状态画成地形：越低的位置越容易被占据。

Definition / 定义

Give every state a number called energy, then draw those numbers as heights. The Boltzmann rule converts those heights into probabilities.

给每个状态一个叫能量的数字，再把这些数字画成高度。玻尔兹曼规则会把高度转换成概率。

\[ p(x)=Z^{-1}e^{-E(x)/T} \]

Example / 例子

In a four-state model, states 00, 01, 10, and 11 can each have different energy. The lower two states become the patterns the model visits more often.

在四状态模型里，00、01、10、11 可以有不同能量；较低的状态会被模型更频繁地访问。

Why it matters / 为什么重要

Learning in a Boltzmann machine is easiest to see as terrain editing: lower the data valleys and raise unsupported regions.

玻尔兹曼机的学习最容易被看成地形编辑：压低数据所在的谷地，抬高缺乏数据支持的区域。
